// what it does

No dashboard to check. It just runs.

→Goes silent right before your 8am class, back to ring the moment it ends.
→Keeps working after a restart — schedules rebuild themselves automatically.
→Three modes, one weekly schedule, zero taps once it's set.
→No account, no cloud sync, no analytics — the schedule lives on your phone.

// how it works

It's one alarm, re-arming itself, forever.

01

An alarm fires at the exact minute you set, and the ringer mode changes.

02

It sets the next one immediately after — same day, next week — before it forgets.

03

If the phone reboots, everything Android wiped gets quietly rebuilt from scratch.

step three loops back into step one — that's the whole trick.
